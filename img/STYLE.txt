tsukijifishmarket.org illustration style (for /chatgpt-image; the main session generates, build agents only plan)

Look: flat editorial illustration, warm paper background (#faf8f4), ink outlines, limited palette of tuna red (#c0392f),
egg yellow (#f2b705), nori dark green-black (#22261f) and, for Toyosu scenes, cool ice blue (#eef4f5 / #2c6e7f).
No photo-realism, no 3D render look, no text or lettering inside the image (labels belong in the HTML caption).

Setting rules:
- Toyosu scenes are INDOORS: a sealed, modern wholesale building, long glass-walled visitor corridors, the trading
  floor seen from above through glass. Visitors never stand on the trading floor.
- Tsukiji scenes are OUTDOORS: narrow open-air lanes of small shop fronts and stalls, daylight.
- Never show: close-ups of knives or blades, blood, a person eating while walking, brand logos, readable signs,
  identifiable real people, Mt Fuji or any landmark not on the page.
- One scene per image. 1200x630 delivered (figimg w/h must match), caption starts "Illustration."
