Want it private, wrapped in a whole Tokyo day?

We deliberately keep generic customisable private tours out of the grid — the market is one optional stop on them, and eleven near-identical listings would bury the genuinely market-focused products. If that format is what you want, the strongest of them is the licensed-guide customised private day (★ 4.8 · 1,608 reviews) — tell the guide the market comes first, before 9 a.m., and read our calendar page so you don't book it onto a closed Wednesday.

How this catalogue is built (and the disclosure)

We scraped every product GetYourGuide files under Tsukiji Outer Market, the Tsukiji neighbourhood, Toyosu Fish Market and Tokyo's fish-and-seafood categories — 133 unique products — then removed anything not genuinely about these markets: category pages get padded with citywide bestsellers, and we caught a Ginza sumo show, a samurai class and a tour of Ibaraki's Oarai market wearing the "fish market" label, plus eleven interchangeable customisable city tours. What's left keeps a 30-review, 4.3★ floor. Cards are ranked by review count; per-card notes are ours, written from the market's own rules and the operators' small print — including which auction listing's terms contradict Tokyo's official lottery schedule.
